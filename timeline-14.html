CURRENT AND HISTORICAL RESTORATIONS

2020-present
Marble and structural replacement
2000s-2010s
Repairs made after devastating storms, pollution, and structural stress
1990s
Large scale cleaning and restoration and pollution control

1947-1980s
Routine maintenance and protection during wars. Managed by the Archaeological Survey of India

1900s-1940s
Periodic repairs to structural elements, especially leaks in the dome

Major restorations after serious disrepair under British rule. Gardens redesigned and interior elements restored
