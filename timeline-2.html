CURRENT AND HISTORICAL RESTORATIONS

2026
Stabilization of the west pediment and facade, scaffolding was recently removed but returned to complete restoration
2011-2026
Removal of earlier iron supports, installation of titanium connectors, and rebuilding interior wall
1900s-2000s
Columns on the North colonnade were dismantled, repaired, and reassembled with marble fragments reintegrated
1983-1991
Dismantling and reassembly of the east side and removal of damaged pieces and replacing iron clamps with titanium ones
1975
Launch of the Acropolis Restoration Project, the first comprehensive conservation program for the monuments on Acropolis
1922-1933
Extensive reconstruction of the North colonnade and structural blocks
1902-1909
Reconstruction of colonnades and pediment elements
1898-1902
Erecting columns and architectural elements. Iron clamps and cement used to connect marble blocks
1841-1844
First partial re-erections of architectural blocks
