CURRENT AND HISTORICAL RESTORATIONS

2020-Present
Ongoing restoration of the facade, including replacing stones
2013-2019
Cleaning and replacing of damaged sandstone blocks on North tower
2004-2011
Cleaning and repair of sculptures and masonry on the South tower
1990s
Exterior stones replaced on structural supports
1960s-1970s
Industrial pollution caused severe blackening and erosion of the stone
1945-1956
Several vaults, windows, and roof sections rebuilt after damage during World War II
1920s-1930s
Repaired buttresses and masonry due to age
1880-1900s
Sandstone facade repaired due to quick deterioration from the weather
1842-1880
Construction resumed following medieval plans after nearly 600 years
