CURRENT AND HISTORICAL RESTORATIONS

2018-present
Continuous structural monitoring
2008
Engineers announce that tower would remain stable for the next 200 years
1990-2001
Tower closed to the public as engineers remove soil and install counterweights, reducing tilt by 40cm
1964
Italian government forms international committee to address growing tilt
1934-1935
Cement grout injected into foundation in an attempt to stabilize the tower as Mussolini Regime sees tower as "national disgrace"
1838
Excavation of tower’s base to expose original marble foundation, accidentally leading to an increase of the tower’s tilt
