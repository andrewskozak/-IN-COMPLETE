CURRENT AND HISTORICAL RESTORATIONS

2020-Present
Continuous structural reinforcement after the building was converted to a mosque. Seismic stabilization and dome restoration.
2016-2020
Preservation of mosaics, repairs to dome, and structural monitoring
2006-2013
Facade cleaning and minaret restoration
1993-1997
Repaired cracks in the dome and reinforcement of structural elements
1930s-1950s
Extensive conservation work that revealed medieval era mosaics
1931-1935
Closed for restoration and conversion to museum
1847-1849
Reinforcing columns, restoring mosaics, and strengthening the dome
